# SplitFair

Build a responsive, anonymous bill-splitting calculator. No account is needed.

The user enters a bill amount, tip percentage, and number of people. Show the
tip amount, total including tip, and amount per person as values change.
Use USD, round displayed money to two decimal places, and explain the inputs.
For example, a $100 bill with a 15% tip split among 4 people costs $28.75 each.
Reject negative amounts, negative tips, and zero or fractional people with
clear inline feedback. A Reset action restores a blank bill, 15% tip, and
one person. Keep the layout readable on phones and support keyboard entry.

This is a single-page tool. No payment collection, user roles, inventory,
approval flow, or external integrations are requested.
