# Team Leads

Build a responsive CRM for a small sales team. A sales representative can
create leads with name, company, email, estimated value, and optional notes.
Leads have New, Contacted, Won, or Lost status. The team can browse and search
leads, filter by status, edit details, and move a lead through these statuses.
Show counts by status and the total value of Won leads. Updated details and
status must persist after a browser refresh.

Use a clearly labeled demo salesperson identity; real authentication and
external email delivery are outside scope. Prepare a few realistic leads in
different statuses. Include useful empty, loading, validation, and error states.
No seed file or numbered acceptance tests are supplied.
